Budget 2027-28: Pre-Budget Consultations Conclude

Posted Date 27 Nov 2026

Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman has completed a series of consultations with industry leaders, experts and representatives from different sectors as the government prepares the Union Budget for 2027-28. More than 200 stakeholders representing 13 sectors and groups participated in the discussions.

The consultation process took place between November 16 and 26, during which the Finance Minister chaired 13 meetings. Participants included industry representatives, economists, academics, professionals, business leaders and youth representatives. This year's consultations also introduced a dedicated interaction with young people for the first time.

Discussions covered a wide range of subjects, including agriculture, rural development, manufacturing, MSMEs, startups, banking, financial services, information technology, infrastructure, energy, employment, housing, healthcare, education and research and development.

Stakeholders shared recommendations focused on improving India's economic competitiveness, developing skilled human resources, encouraging innovation, increasing agricultural productivity and creating more employment opportunities.

Focus on Agriculture, Manufacturing and AI

Among the proposals discussed was the need to strengthen domestic manufacturing of biofertilisers and biotechnology-based agricultural inputs. Participants also suggested developing sector-specific Artificial Intelligence (AI) models designed to work efficiently with locally available hardware, helping build India's domestic AI capabilities.

Improving cybersecurity awareness among micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs), as well as smaller financial institutions, was another important recommendation. Stakeholders highlighted the need to improve cyber-hygiene practices to reduce digital security risks.

Participants also called for greater support to help rural youth and workers transition into non-farm employment and establish sustainable businesses, creating additional income opportunities in rural areas.

Education and Affordable Housing Among Key Suggestions

In the education sector, stakeholders proposed launching a nationwide reading initiative to ensure that every child can read and understand a newspaper by the end of Grade 5. The suggestion aims to strengthen basic reading and comprehension skills among schoolchildren.

On housing, participants recommended giving greater attention to rental housing to expand affordable accommodation options in urban areas. They also stressed the importance of accelerating the development of Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.

Developing smaller cities could create new opportunities for employment, entrepreneurship and investment, particularly benefiting young people seeking opportunities beyond major metropolitan centres.

Senior Finance Ministry Officials Attend Consultations

The meetings were also attended by Economic Affairs Secretary Anuradha Thakur, Chief Economic Adviser V. Anantha Nageswaran, senior officials from the Finance Ministry and secretaries from the concerned government departments.

The recommendations received during the consultations will contribute to the government's preparation of the Union Budget 2027-28. The final budget announcements will determine which proposals, if any, are incorporated into the government's financial and policy priorities for the coming financial year.
