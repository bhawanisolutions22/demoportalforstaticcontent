India’s Net Direct Tax Collections Grow 11.8% to ₹14.42 Lakh Crore in FY27

India’s net direct tax collections recorded strong growth during the current financial year, rising 11.76% year-on-year to ₹14.42 lakh crore as of November 10, FY27, according to figures released by the Income Tax Department.

During the corresponding period of the previous financial year, net direct tax collections stood at ₹12.90 lakh crore, indicating a significant increase in tax receipts.

Gross direct tax collections, before accounting for refunds, also registered healthy growth. Collections increased 14.02% to ₹17.50 lakh crore, compared with ₹15.35 lakh crore recorded during the same period last year.

Corporate and Non-Corporate Tax Collections

Net corporate tax collections climbed to ₹6.32 lakh crore, up from ₹5.36 lakh crore in the corresponding period of the previous financial year.

Securities Transaction Tax (STT) recorded particularly strong growth, with collections jumping 51.56% to ₹54,077.90 crore, compared with ₹35,681.88 crore a year earlier.

Net non-corporate tax collections, which include payments from individuals, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), firms and other entities, increased to ₹7.56 lakh crore from ₹7.18 lakh crore during the same period last year.

Tax Refunds Increase by Nearly 26%

The government also reported a rise in tax refunds during the current financial year. Direct tax refunds issued up to November 10 increased by nearly 26% to ₹3.08 lakh crore.

The reported direct tax figures cover collections from companies, individuals, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), firms, associations of persons, bodies of individuals, local authorities and artificial juridical persons.

Government Achieves 53% of FY27 Tax Collection Target

For FY27, the government has set a direct tax collection target of ₹26.97 lakh crore, representing a 15% increase over the ₹23.40 lakh crore collected during FY26.

With net collections reaching ₹14.42 lakh crore by November 10, the government has already achieved approximately 53% of its annual direct tax revenue target.

The latest figures indicate continued growth in India's direct tax receipts, supported by collections from both corporate and non-corporate taxpayers. The remaining months of FY27 will be important in determining whether the government can meet its full-year collection target.
