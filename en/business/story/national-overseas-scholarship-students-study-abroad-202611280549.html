National Overseas Scholarship for ST Students

Posted Date 28 Nov 2026

The Government of India's National Overseas Scholarship (NOS) Scheme is opening international higher education opportunities for meritorious students belonging to Scheduled Tribes (STs). The programme provides financial assistance to eligible candidates pursuing Master's and PhD degrees at reputed universities and institutions outside India.

Under the scheme, 20 fresh scholarships are offered every year. To promote inclusive access to global education, 30% of the scholarships are earmarked for women, while three scholarships are reserved for candidates belonging to Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs).

Implemented by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs as a Central Sector Scheme since 1954-55, the programme has gradually expanded its scope. From the academic year 2026-27, students can receive support for postgraduate and doctoral studies at foreign institutions featuring among the top 1,000 in the latest QS World University Rankings.

Here is a detailed explanation of the scholarship, including its benefits, eligibility conditions, selection procedure and application process.

1. What is the National Overseas Scholarship Scheme?

The National Overseas Scholarship Scheme helps eligible ST students pursue higher education abroad by covering major educational and living expenses.

The financial assistance includes tuition fees, maintenance allowances, international travel and other approved expenses. The programme offers 20 new scholarships annually for Master's and PhD courses.

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs implements the scheme in coordination with Indian Embassies and Missions abroad through the Ministry of External Affairs.

Its coverage has expanded over the years. Initially, in 2010-11, the scheme offered 15 scholarships primarily for engineering, technology and science. It now provides 20 scholarships across four broad academic disciplines.

2. Which courses and subjects are covered?

From the 2026-27 academic year, eligible students can pursue Master's and PhD programmes at foreign universities and institutions ranked among the top 1,000 in the latest QS World University Rankings.

The annual allocation of 20 scholarships is divided into four academic categories:

  • Science, Engineering, Technology and Mathematics (STEM): 10 scholarships.
  • Management, Economics, Finance and Law: 4 scholarships.
  • Agriculture and Medicine: 4 scholarships.
  • Humanities, Social Sciences and Fine Arts: 2 scholarships.

Out of the total 20 scholarships, 17 are allocated to ST candidates, while three are specifically reserved for students from Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs).

This dedicated allocation aims to improve access to international education for tribal communities facing greater social and economic disadvantages.

3. How does the scheme encourage women students?

Promoting higher education among tribal women is another important objective of the National Overseas Scholarship Scheme.

Under its guidelines, 30% of the available scholarships are reserved for female candidates. This provision is intended to improve women's participation in international education and advanced research.

The participation of women among selected beneficiaries has increased considerably. During 2015-16, three out of 15 selected candidates were women, representing 20% of the total.

By 2024-25, the number had increased to 11 out of 23 selections, taking women's representation to approximately 47.8%.

If the earmarked scholarships for women remain vacant, the available slots can be offered to eligible male candidates.

4. What is the duration of scholarship assistance?

The scholarship provides financial support for the prescribed duration of the selected academic programme or until the course or research is completed, whichever is earlier.

The maximum period of financial assistance is:

  • PhD programmes: Up to four years.
  • Master's programmes: One or two years, depending on the course duration.

Students should plan their overseas studies within the prescribed period to ensure that they can make full use of the available financial assistance.

5. Who is eligible for the National Overseas Scholarship?

Applicants must satisfy the academic qualification, age and family-income requirements prescribed under the scheme.

Eligibility for PhD applicants

  • A relevant Master's degree with at least 55% marks or an equivalent grade.
  • Maximum age of 35 years as on July 1 of the selection year.

Eligibility for Master's applicants

  • A relevant Bachelor's degree with at least 55% marks or an equivalent grade.
  • Maximum age of 32 years as on July 1 of the selection year.

Family income requirement

The applicant's total annual family income from all sources must not exceed ₹6 lakh.

The scheme also follows a one-child-per-family condition. Only one child from a family can receive the scholarship, subject to the applicant's self-certification.

Additionally, a candidate can benefit from the scholarship only once and cannot receive another award under the scheme at the same or a higher academic level.

6. How are candidates selected?

Students interested in the scheme must independently apply for admission to eligible foreign universities and institutions.

From the 2026-27 academic year, priority is given to applicants who have already received an admission offer from an institution ranked among the top 1,000 in the latest QS World University Rankings but have not joined the institution by the application date.

The merit list is primarily prepared according to the international ranking of the institution offering admission.

Where two or more applicants have offers from institutions with the same ranking, their marks or grades in the qualifying examination are considered to determine their position.

If scholarships remain available after this process, additional candidates may be selected through personal interviews conducted by an expert committee established by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs.

Preference is also given to candidates who have qualified examinations such as:

  • GRE (Graduate Record Examinations).
  • GMAT (Graduate Management Admission Test).
  • TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language).

After receiving the award letter, selected candidates must secure admission and join an eligible institution within two years.

7. What financial benefits does the scholarship provide?

The National Overseas Scholarship covers several essential expenses associated with studying in another country.

Annual maintenance allowance

  • United States: USD 15,400.
  • United Kingdom: GBP 9,900.
  • Other countries: Applicable allowance in US dollars or equivalent rates.

Contingency and equipment allowance

Students are also eligible for an annual contingency allowance of USD 1,532 or GBP 1,116.

This amount can be used for approved academic requirements, including books, essential equipment, study tours, typing and thesis binding.

Tuition and other compulsory fees

The scheme covers tuition fees and other compulsory, non-refundable charges imposed by the educational institution.

It also provides assistance for actual visa fees and applicable poll taxes.

Medical insurance and incidental expenses

Medical insurance premiums are reimbursed at actual rates, subject to assessment by the concerned Indian Embassy or Mission.

Students can also receive incidental journey expenses of up to USD 20 or its equivalent in Indian rupees.

8. Does the scholarship cover international travel?

Yes. The scheme provides economy-class airfare for eligible students travelling from India to the nearest destination serving their educational institution and returning to India.

Airfare is reimbursed at actual cost, subject to travel through the shortest available route and the best available fare.

The programme also covers eligible local transportation between the arrival point and the place of study.

This includes second-class railway fares. Where railway connectivity is unavailable, admissible expenses for bus, ferry or connecting air travel may also be covered under the prescribed guidelines.

9. How can students apply for the scholarship?

Applications are accepted online through the dedicated National Overseas Scholarship Portal.

The portal provides access to scheme guidelines, application procedures and the list of documents required from applicants.

An important feature of the platform is its integration with DigiLocker, allowing students to retrieve available documents digitally and upload additional documents whenever necessary.

The portal also offers facilities for:

  • Online submission of scholarship applications.
  • Access to scheme guidelines and eligibility requirements.
  • Submission of queries and grievances.
  • SMS and email notifications about application updates.
  • Monitoring applications through an integrated dashboard.

Students should regularly check the official portal for announcements regarding application dates and selection rounds.

10. What is the scheme's coverage and financial progress?

The National Overseas Scholarship Scheme has nationwide coverage. Eligible ST students from all States and Union Territories can apply, as there is no state-wise or district-wise allocation of scholarships or funds.

Government expenditure under the programme has increased significantly over the years.

Financial disbursements rose from ₹0.39 crore in 2015-16 to ₹8 crore in 2025-26.

The number of students supported annually, including new and continuing beneficiaries, also increased from eight in 2013-14 to 64 in 2025-26.

Overall, approximately 400 students received assistance during the period covered by the reported figures. Since 2014-15, the Ministry of Tribal Affairs has released ₹46.46 crore under the scheme.

The expansion of the programme coincides with rising higher education participation among ST communities.

According to the All India Survey on Higher Education (AISHE) 2023-24, the number of ST students enrolled in higher education reached 28,83,218, compared with 21,56,109 in 2019-20.

Expanding global education opportunities for tribal students

The National Overseas Scholarship Scheme is an important initiative aimed at making international higher education more accessible to deserving students from Scheduled Tribe communities.

By supporting tuition fees, living expenses, travel and other essential costs, the programme helps reduce the financial challenges associated with studying abroad.

Its dedicated provisions for women and Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups further strengthen its inclusive approach.

As more tribal students gain access to international universities, advanced research and specialised academic opportunities, the scheme can contribute to greater professional mobility, social inclusion and India's broader vision of Viksit Bharat.
