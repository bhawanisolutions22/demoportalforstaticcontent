FPIs Invest ₹38,971 Crore in Indian Equities as Foreign Investors Return

Posted Date 09 Nov 2026

Foreign portfolio investors (FPIs) have made a strong comeback in Indian equity markets, investing ₹38,971 crore in a single week, according to data released by the National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL). The substantial inflow signals renewed foreign interest in Indian stocks amid growing uncertainty in global technology markets.

The return of foreign investment comes at a time when international technology stocks are facing a significant correction. Concerns over the long-term profitability of artificial intelligence (AI) investments and the sustainability of massive data centre spending have prompted investors to reassess their global portfolios.

Earlier, foreign investors had reduced their exposure to Indian equities as substantial capital moved towards markets benefiting from the global AI investment boom. However, the recent decline in technology stocks appears to have encouraged investors to reconsider India as an attractive investment destination.

Global markets experienced heavy selling pressure on Thursday and Friday, with US technology companies bearing much of the impact. All three major US stock indices recorded their biggest single-day declines since April 2025 on Thursday, erasing gains made during the previous day's post-election rally.

Foreign and Domestic Investment Trends Reverse

V K Vijayakumar, Chief Investment Strategist at Geojit Investments Limited, highlighted a significant shift in institutional investment activity during the week.

According to Vijayakumar, foreign portfolio investors turned aggressive buyers, while domestic institutional investors (DIIs) remained net sellers throughout the trading sessions.

He linked the reversal in investment patterns to corrections in AI-focused markets, particularly in the United States, South Korea, Taiwan and Japan. Growing concerns about the scale and financial sustainability of data centre investments have contributed to the recent market volatility.

Vijayakumar noted that India's relatively limited exposure to the AI-driven investment rally, combined with healthy corporate earnings growth and attractive valuations among large-cap companies, has improved the appeal of Indian equities for overseas investors.

Nifty Gains 7.58% Amid Aggressive FPI Buying

The return of foreign capital provided significant support to domestic stock markets. Aggressive FPI purchases, along with a short squeeze in the market, contributed to a sharp rally in Indian equities.

The benchmark Nifty 50 index gained 7.58 per cent during the week, reflecting the strength of the recovery.

Foreign investors concentrated their purchases in several heavyweight stocks, particularly across the financial services, information technology, automobile and telecommunications sectors.

India Gains Attention as Global Investors Reassess AI Investments

The latest investment trend highlights how changing global market conditions can influence capital flows into emerging economies.

As investors reassess the pace, costs and long-term returns of AI infrastructure development, Indian equities appear to be attracting renewed attention.

Strong corporate earnings, relatively attractive large-cap valuations and India's different exposure to the global technology investment cycle have contributed to its appeal.

The ₹38,971 crore weekly inflow marks a notable return of foreign institutional buying. Whether this momentum continues will depend on global market stability, corporate earnings performance and investors' changing assessment of opportunities across international markets.
