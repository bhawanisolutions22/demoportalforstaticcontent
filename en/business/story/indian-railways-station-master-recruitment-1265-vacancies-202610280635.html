Indian Railways Station Master Recruitment 2026: 1,265 Vacancies Announced

Indian Railways has announced recruitment for 1,265 Station Master posts across the country, offering an opportunity for eligible graduate candidates to apply for positions within the railway network.

According to a press release from the Ministry of Railways, the recruitment drive is part of the department’s efforts to strengthen its operational workforce and expand employment opportunities for qualified candidates.

Separate Station Master Recruitment from 2026

The Railway Board has decided to issue separate recruitment notifications for Station Master posts from 2026 onwards. The dedicated process is intended to provide candidates with specific information about eligibility criteria, examination procedures, vacancies and application requirements.

Applicants should carefully review the official notification to understand the selection process and ensure they meet the prescribed conditions before applying.

RRB-Wise Station Master Vacancy Details

A total of 1,265 vacancies have been distributed across various Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) and railway zones. This allows eligible candidates to compete for positions in different parts of India.

According to the vacancy information provided, the three RRBs with the highest numbers of openings are:

  • RRB Mumbai: 177 vacancies
  • RRB Jammu-Srinagar: 122 vacancies
  • RRB Chennai: 119 vacancies

Candidates should consult the official notification for the complete RRB-wise vacancy breakdown and other applicable recruitment details.

Important Dates for Station Master Recruitment 2026

The online application process is scheduled to open on October 28, 2026, and close on November 25, 2026.

Eligible candidates are advised to complete their applications before the deadline. They should check the official recruitment notice for the prescribed educational qualifications, other eligibility conditions, examination syllabus, application procedure and any applicable instructions.

How to Apply for Railway Station Master Posts

Candidates can use the following official resources to access the recruitment notification and application portal:

  • Official RRB website https://rrb.indianrailways.gov.in/
  • Online application portal: https://www.rrbapply.gov.in/

Applicants should rely on official Railway Recruitment Board announcements for the latest information about eligibility, vacancies, examinations and application-related updates.

Key Takeaway

The Indian Railways Station Master recruitment drive offers 1,265 vacancies for eligible graduates, with RRB Mumbai, Jammu-Srinagar and Chennai among the boards reporting the highest vacancy numbers. Candidates interested in these posts should read the detailed notification and submit their applications within the announced dates.

Note: Candidates should verify all eligibility requirements, vacancy details and dates against the official CEN No. 08/2026 notification before applying.
