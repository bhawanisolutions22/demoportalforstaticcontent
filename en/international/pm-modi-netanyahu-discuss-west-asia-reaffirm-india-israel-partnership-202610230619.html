PM Modi calls for affordable, accessible technology to empower Global South nations

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu held a telephone conversation on Thursday to discuss the latest developments in West Asia and reaffirm their commitment to strengthening the India-Israel Special Strategic Partnership.

According to a statement issued by the Prime Minister’s Office (PMO), the two leaders expressed satisfaction with the continued progress in bilateral relations and agreed to further expand cooperation between the two countries.

The conversation also focused on the evolving situation in West Asia, with both leaders highlighting the importance of restoring peace and stability across the region.

Prime Minister Modi reiterated India’s position that dialogue and diplomacy remain essential to resolving regional challenges and achieving lasting peace.

The two leaders agreed to maintain contact and continue discussions on bilateral relations and regional developments.
