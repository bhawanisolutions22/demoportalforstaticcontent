India and Myanmar Launch First Tri-Services HADR Exercise RAHAT MITRA in Chennai

India and Myanmar have launched their first-ever bilateral Tri-Services Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR) exercise, named RAHAT MITRA, in Chennai. The initiative marks an important development in defence cooperation between the neighbouring countries, with a focus on strengthening their ability to coordinate during humanitarian emergencies and natural disasters.

The two-day exercise commenced on November 5 and concluded on November 6, bringing together military personnel from the Army, Navy and Air Force of both countries.

Organised around the theme "Together for Humanity, Stronger through Partnership," RAHAT MITRA aims to improve mutual understanding, build trust and enhance operational coordination between the participating armed forces.

More than 300 personnel from India and Myanmar are taking part in the exercise. It provides an opportunity for military teams to exchange professional knowledge, share operational experiences and develop common approaches to humanitarian assistance and disaster relief missions.

The Indian Navy is coordinating the exercise, which focuses on improving the preparedness of both countries to respond effectively to emergencies. Through joint activities and professional interactions, the participating forces are working to strengthen interoperability and develop a better understanding of each other's disaster-response capabilities.

The launch of RAHAT MITRA also represents a significant step in the broader India-Myanmar defence and security partnership. As neighbouring countries with shared regional interests, both nations recognise the importance of cooperation in responding to natural disasters and other humanitarian challenges.

The initiative is aligned with India's Neighbourhood First policy and its wider regional vision of MAHASAGAR (Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions).

By bringing their three military services together on a common platform, India and Myanmar have reaffirmed their commitment to regional cooperation, peace and stability, as well as providing humanitarian assistance when needed.

The maiden RAHAT MITRA exercise highlights the growing importance of joint disaster-response capabilities and defence partnerships in addressing humanitarian challenges across the region.
