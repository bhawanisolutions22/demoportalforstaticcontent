PM Modi, Trump Reaffirm Commitment to Strengthen India‑US Strategic Partnership

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and U.S. President Donald Trump held a telephone conversation on Wednesday to review progress in bilateral cooperation across key sectors including trade, defence, energy, and critical technologies. The discussion underscored the shared resolve to advance the India‑US Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership for the benefit of both nations.

According to a statement from the Prime Minister’s Office, both leaders agreed to maintain close engagement and deepen collaboration in areas of mutual interest. They also exchanged views on regional and global developments, reaffirming their commitment to promoting peace and security worldwide.

PM Modi later posted on X:

“Had a productive conversation with President Trump. We reviewed our bilateral cooperation in trade, defence, energy, critical technologies and other areas. We agreed to maintain close engagement to take our Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership forward. We also exchanged views on regional and international issues, including ongoing efforts to advance global peace and security.”
