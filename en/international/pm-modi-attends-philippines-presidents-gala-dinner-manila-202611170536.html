PM Modi Attends Gala Dinner Hosted by Philippines President in Manila

Posted Date 17 Nov 2026

Prime Minister Narendra Modi attended a gala dinner hosted by Philippine President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. in Manila on Monday, November 16, during his visit to the Philippines for the ASEAN Summit and related high-level meetings.

The dinner took place at the Philippine International Convention Centre and brought together visiting leaders and dignitaries. For the occasion, Prime Minister Modi wore the Barong Tagalog, the traditional formal attire for Filipino men.

The evening also featured Filipino cultural performances and traditional cuisine, highlighting the country's rich cultural heritage.

PM Modi Begins Visit to Attend ASEAN and East Asia Summits

Prime Minister Modi arrived in Manila at the invitation of President Marcos to participate in the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and the 21st East Asia Summit, scheduled for November 17.

He was received at the airport by Robert Joseph 'Jay' Montes de Claro, Secretary of the Philippines' Department of Information and Communications Technology.

Following his arrival at the hotel, the Prime Minister received a warm welcome from members of the Indian community living in the Philippines. Local artists and community members organised traditional music and dance performances to mark the occasion.

Modi appreciated the diaspora's efforts to preserve Indian culture while helping strengthen relations between the two countries. In a post on X, he said he was happy to meet the community and described its contribution to bringing India and the Philippines closer.

Cultural Performances Highlight India-Philippines Connections

The welcome programme included children from both countries reciting the Purna Kumbha Shloka, along with Bollywood songs and Bhangra performances.

A special presentation of the Ramayana was also organised by the Integrated Performing Arts Guild of Iligan City, which presented the epic with its distinctive traditional interpretation.

During the programme, the Prime Minister also viewed replicas of the Golden Tara of Agusan and the historic Laguna Copperplate Inscription, artefacts associated with the Philippines' historical and cultural connections with the wider Asian region.

Modi described the cultural programme as a reflection of the deep-rooted cultural links between India and the Philippines.

India Looks to Strengthen Its ASEAN Partnership

Before departing for Manila, the Prime Minister highlighted the importance of ASEAN countries in India's Act East Policy.

He noted that India's relationship with the regional grouping has developed over centuries of civilisational exchanges and now covers several important areas, including trade, connectivity, maritime security, technology and people-to-people cooperation.

At the ASEAN-India Summit, Modi is expected to work with fellow leaders to advance the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership and strengthen cooperation in areas of shared interest.

The East Asia Summit will provide another important platform for discussions on regional and international developments. The Prime Minister said he looked forward to exchanging views with participating leaders on promoting peace, stability and cooperation across the Indo-Pacific region.

His visit reflects India's continued engagement with ASEAN and its efforts to expand strategic, economic and cultural cooperation with countries across Southeast Asia.
