India and Sri Lanka Launch 3,500-km NCC Maitri Setu Cycling Expedition 2027

A 3,500-kilometre cycling expedition bringing together young cadets from India and Sri Lanka has been launched to strengthen friendship, cooperation and cultural ties between the two neighbouring countries.

Named the Maitri Setu Yatra – India-Sri Lanka NCC Cycling Expedition 2027, the initiative will bring together 24 participants, comprising 14 Indian and 10 Sri Lankan National Cadet Corps (NCC) personnel. The team is expected to cover approximately 3,500 kilometres over a journey lasting more than 75 days, travelling from Colombo to New Delhi.

The expedition was officially flagged off in Colombo in the presence of India's High Commissioner to Sri Lanka, Santosh Jha, and Sri Lanka's Sports Minister, Sunil Kumar Gamage.

Addressing the participating cadets, High Commissioner Santosh Jha said the journey would carry a broader message of peace, friendship, cooperation and mutual respect. He noted that every kilometre covered by the cyclists would represent the strong bonds shared by the two neighbouring nations.

Sri Lankan Sports Minister Sunil Kumar Gamage also appreciated the National Cadet Corps of both countries and the Ministry of Defence for organising the initiative. He described the expedition as a meaningful effort to promote friendship and closer relations between India and Sri Lanka.

Expedition to Cover Historic Destinations and 11 Indian States

The cycling journey will begin with an approximately 500-kilometre route across Sri Lanka, taking the participants through historically significant destinations, including Anuradhapura and Jaffna.

After completing the Sri Lankan leg, the contingent will travel by sea to India. From there, the cadets will undertake another 3,000 kilometres of cycling across 11 Indian states and Union Territories before reaching New Delhi.

The expedition is scheduled to conclude in January 2027 at the NCC Prime Minister's Rally in the national capital.

Strengthening India-Sri Lanka People-to-People Relations

Beyond its sporting and endurance challenges, the Maitri Setu Yatra is intended to encourage stronger people-to-people connections, particularly among the younger generations of both countries.

The initiative also highlights the centuries-old historical and cultural links between India and Sri Lanka. Shared Buddhist heritage, traditional maritime trade routes and longstanding civilisational connections have contributed to the relationship between the two nations.

By bringing together young cadets from both sides, the expedition aims to turn these historical connections into stronger contemporary partnerships built on friendship, understanding and mutual respect.

The Maitri Setu Yatra therefore represents more than a long-distance cycling challenge. It is an effort to promote cross-border friendship and demonstrate how youth-led initiatives can contribute to closer relations between neighbouring countries.
