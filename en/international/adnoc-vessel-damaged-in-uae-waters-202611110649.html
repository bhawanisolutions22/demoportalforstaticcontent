ADNOC Vessel Damaged in Attack in UAE Territorial Waters

A vessel operated by the logistics division of the United Arab Emirates' national oil company, ADNOC, has sustained damage following an attack in the country's territorial waters in the Arabian Gulf.

ADNOC Logistics and Services confirmed the incident, stating that one of its vessels was damaged during an attack on Tuesday afternoon.

In a statement carried by the Emirates News Agency (WAM), the company said that the relevant authorities and response teams were working to address the situation.

"Response efforts by the relevant teams are ongoing, and further updates will be provided as additional information becomes available," the company said.

The company has not yet disclosed the name or type of vessel involved. Details regarding the extent of the damage, the circumstances surrounding the attack and whether there were any casualties have also not been released.

ADNOC Logistics and Services is the maritime and logistics arm of the Abu Dhabi National Oil Company. It provides shipping, integrated logistics and marine support services to the energy sector, playing an important role in the UAE's oil and gas transportation network.

The incident comes amid continued attention on maritime security in the strategically important Arabian Gulf, a major international route for energy transportation. Further details are awaited as response efforts continue.
