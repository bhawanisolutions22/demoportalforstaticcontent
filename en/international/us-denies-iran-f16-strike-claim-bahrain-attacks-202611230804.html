US Denies Iranian Claim of F-16 Strike as Bahrain Reports Continued Attacks

Posted Date 23 Nov 2026

The United States has rejected Iranian claims that its Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) struck and damaged a US F-16 fighter jet in the Middle East. Iranian state media had earlier reported that the aircraft was targeted and forced to make an emergency landing.

However, the US Central Command (CENTCOM) dismissed the report in a social media statement, saying that Iran had not attacked any US F-16 fighter aircraft. CENTCOM also confirmed that all American military fighter jets operating in the Middle East remained safe and accounted for.

Bahrain Reports Attacks on Civilian and Residential Areas

Meanwhile, Bahrain continued to report Iranian attacks on its territory. The country's Ministry of Interior said that an Iranian strike targeted a civilian industrial facility, triggering a fire at the site. Although the incident caused material damage, authorities confirmed that no injuries were reported.

The Bahrain Defence Force also stated that its air defence systems intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks on Saturday. Debris from the interceptions reportedly fell in residential areas, causing minor property damage. No casualties were reported in these incidents either.

Saudi Arabia Intercepts Missile and Drone Attacks

Saudi Arabia also reported fresh security incidents involving attacks attributed to the Houthis. According to the Saudi-led coalition, air defence systems intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile over the country's western region.

The coalition also confirmed the interception and destruction of a drone launched towards the Riyadh area.

In the early hours of Sunday, Saudi Arabia's National Early Warning Platform issued an alert for Makkah Province, warning residents of a potential threat.

However, the Houthis denied posing any threat to Makkah. Houthi-aligned Al Masirah TV quoted the group's military spokesperson as saying that their operations were directed exclusively at Saudi oil facilities and military installations. The spokesperson also stated that the group holds Islamic holy sites in the highest regard.

Regional Security Concerns Continue

The latest developments highlight continuing security concerns across the Middle East, with reported aerial attacks, missile interceptions and conflicting claims from the parties involved. While the US has rejected Iran's claim regarding its F-16 aircraft, Bahrain and Saudi Arabia have reported separate security incidents involving incoming threats.

The situation remains closely watched as regional tensions continue.
