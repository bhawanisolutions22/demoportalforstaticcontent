PM Modi and German Chancellor Merz Discuss Stronger Strategic Ties

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and German Chancellor Friedrich Merz have discussed ways to further strengthen the strategic partnership between India and Germany, with particular emphasis on defence, advanced technology, trade and investment.

The two leaders held a telephone conversation on Tuesday, during which they reviewed the progress of bilateral relations. Both expressed satisfaction with the continued momentum in cooperation and regular high-level engagements between the two countries.

As India and Germany mark 75 years of diplomatic relations in 2026, the leaders reaffirmed their commitment to expanding cooperation across several important sectors. They also highlighted the opportunities that could emerge from the India-European Union Free Trade Agreement, particularly in boosting trade, investment and economic ties.

The discussion also covered regional and international developments of mutual interest. Modi and Merz agreed on the importance of working together to promote global peace, stability and a rules-based international order.

Both leaders further stressed the need for closer coordination at multilateral forums to address emerging global challenges.

The conversation reflects the continued efforts by India and Germany to deepen their longstanding partnership and expand cooperation in areas that are important for economic growth, technological progress and international stability.
