Motorists across the United Arab Emirates (UAE) will receive some relief in November as the government has announced reductions in petrol and diesel prices, reversing the sharp increase recorded in October.

The UAE's Gasoline and Diesel Prices Follow-up Committee has approved a 5.6% reduction in petrol prices and a 1.04% cut in diesel prices for November 2026. The revised rates will come into effect on November 1 across the emirates.

The reduction comes at a time when international crude oil markets continue to experience volatility. Geopolitical tensions and regional supply disruptions have kept oil prices elevated. On Friday, Brent crude reached a six-month high of around $118 per barrel, while West Texas Intermediate (WTI) settled above $104.

New UAE Petrol and Diesel Prices for November 2026

Under the revised pricing structure, all major petrol grades will become cheaper compared with October. Diesel prices will also see a modest reduction.

The updated rates are as follows:

  • Super 98 petrol: AED 4.16 per litre, down from AED 4.40.
  • Special 95 petrol: AED 4.04 per litre, compared with AED 4.28 previously.
  • E-Plus 91 petrol: AED 3.97 per litre, reduced from AED 4.21.
  • Diesel: AED 4.75 per litre, down from AED 4.80.

The reduction is expected to ease fuel expenses for private vehicle owners, commuters and businesses that depend on road transportation.

How the UAE Determines Monthly Fuel Prices

The UAE has followed a market-linked fuel pricing system since August 2015, when the government deregulated petrol and diesel prices.

Under this arrangement, domestic fuel rates are adjusted according to movements in international fuel markets. A designated committee reviews market conditions every month and announces the prices applicable for the following month.

The policy was introduced to bring domestic fuel prices in line with international market trends while encouraging more efficient energy consumption.

Relief After Three Consecutive Monthly Increases

The November reduction follows three consecutive months of fuel price increases, with October recording a particularly sharp rise.

Fuel price announcements are closely followed by UAE residents because petrol and diesel are regular household and business expenses. Changes in fuel costs can influence monthly transportation budgets as well as operating expenses for logistics and other transport-dependent industries.

Although global crude oil prices remain under pressure from geopolitical developments and supply concerns, the latest UAE announcement brings some relief to motorists at the beginning of November.

The revised prices will apply from November 1, 2026, giving drivers across the country an opportunity to benefit from lower fuel costs compared with the previous month.
