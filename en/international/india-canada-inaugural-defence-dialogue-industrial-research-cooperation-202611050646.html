India and Canada Launch First Defence Dialogue, Focus on Industrial and Research Cooperation

India and Canada have held their first-ever Defence Dialogue, opening a new channel for strengthening bilateral defence ties. The virtual meeting took place on November 3, with both countries identifying defence industry partnerships, military-technical engagement and research and development as key areas for future cooperation.

The inaugural dialogue was co-chaired by Amit Telang, Joint Secretary for International Cooperation at India's Ministry of Defence, and Scott Millar, Assistant Deputy Minister for Policy at Canada's Department of National Defence.

During the discussions, officials from both countries exchanged views on their respective defence policies and strategic priorities. They also explored opportunities to expand cooperation across various defence-related sectors, according to a statement issued by India's Ministry of Defence.

Defence industrial collaboration featured prominently in the discussions. Both sides highlighted the potential for closer engagement in military technology, defence research and development, and industrial partnerships.

The meeting also reflected the broader commitment of both governments to strengthening their strategic relationship. This commitment was reaffirmed in the joint statement issued by Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney on March 2, 2026.

In addition to discussing defence cooperation, the two delegations exchanged perspectives on important regional and international developments. They also considered specific initiatives and possible outcomes that could help translate their discussions into practical cooperation.

The inaugural dialogue marks another step in efforts to deepen India-Canada defence engagement. Both countries agreed to maintain regular communication and continue working on the proposals discussed during the meeting to advance their bilateral defence partnership.
