India and France Strengthen Cooperation on Climate Finance and COP31

India and France have discussed expanding their cooperation on climate action, biodiversity conservation, renewable energy and climate finance, while identifying key priorities ahead of the COP31 climate conference.

The discussions took place on Tuesday during a meeting between India's Minister of State for External Affairs, Kirti Vardhan Singh, and France's Minister Delegate for the Ecological Transition, Mathieu Lefevre, in New Delhi.

Following the meeting, Singh said both countries exchanged views on several areas of mutual interest, including biodiversity protection, the transition to clean energy, climate finance and preparations for COP31.

He also reaffirmed India's commitment to strengthening its partnership with France through two major international initiatives: the International Solar Alliance (ISA) and the Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure (CDRI).

France Re-elected Co-President of the International Solar Alliance

France was re-elected as co-president of the International Solar Alliance on Tuesday, reinforcing its continued engagement in international efforts to expand solar energy adoption.

Reacting to the development, Lefevre welcomed the re-election and highlighted France's support for the alliance since 2018. He credited French President Emmanuel Macron's leadership for strengthening the country's commitment to promoting solar energy worldwide.

The International Solar Alliance brings together countries to encourage investment, improve access to affordable solar power, develop technical capabilities and accelerate the global transition towards renewable energy.

India Hosts Ninth ISA Assembly in New Delhi

The ninth session of the International Solar Alliance Assembly is being held in New Delhi alongside the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit 2026.

The meeting is being co-chaired by French Minister Mathieu Lefevre and India's Union Minister Pralhad Joshi. More than 120 countries are participating in discussions focused on expanding affordable solar energy, developing skills and mobilising investment for renewable energy projects.

The French Embassy in India described the gathering as an opportunity for nations to work together towards scaling up solar power and advancing the global clean energy transition.

Macron-Modi Partnership Supports Clean Energy Cooperation

Before the ISA Assembly, Lefevre attended the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit, where he also met Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

He described the meeting as an opportunity to reaffirm France's commitment to accelerating the energy transition through its strategic partnership with India.

France and India have continued to work together on renewable energy, climate action and sustainable development through bilateral cooperation as well as international platforms.

Focus on Renewable Energy and Climate Action

The latest discussions highlight the importance both countries attach to addressing climate change through international cooperation.

By working together through the International Solar Alliance and the Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure, India and France aim to support wider access to clean energy, strengthen climate resilience and encourage investment in sustainable infrastructure.

Their discussions on climate finance, biodiversity and COP31 priorities also underline the broader scope of their partnership as countries prepare for the next phase of global climate negotiations.
