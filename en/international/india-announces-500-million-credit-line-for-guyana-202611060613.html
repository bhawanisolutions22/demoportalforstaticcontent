India Announces $500 Million Credit Line for Guyana

India has announced a new $500 million Line of Credit for Guyana to support development projects in roads, healthcare and defence, following a meeting between External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar and Guyanese President Irfaan Ali at the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM) 2026 in Antigua and Barbuda.

During their meeting on Thursday, the two leaders reviewed the progress of bilateral relations and discussed opportunities to expand cooperation across several sectors. They also took stock of developments since Prime Minister Narendra Modi's visit to Guyana in November 2024.

Sharing details of the meeting on social media platform X, Jaishankar said both sides had discussed the steady progress in their partnership. He also confirmed the extension of the $500 million credit facility to support infrastructure, healthcare and defence-related projects in Guyana.

India and Guyana Sign Three Cooperation Agreements

Alongside the announcement of the credit facility, India and Guyana signed three agreements aimed at strengthening economic and technological cooperation.

The agreements cover the supply and installation of manufacturing projects for micro and small enterprises (MSEs), grant assistance for implementing Quick Impact Projects (QIPs), and collaboration in Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI).

An important aspect of the digital cooperation agreement is the development and functioning of a Unified Payments Interface (UPI)-like payment system in Guyana. The initiative is expected to support the expansion of digital payment services and strengthen the country's digital infrastructure.

Jaishankar highlighted the agreements in a separate post on X, describing them as important areas of cooperation between the two countries.

Jaishankar Holds Meetings with Australian and UK Leaders

On the sidelines of CHOGM 2026, Jaishankar also held discussions with Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese as part of India's wider diplomatic engagements at the summit.

He separately met United Kingdom Prime Minister Andy Burnham. Their discussions focused on strengthening economic relations under the Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA), along with an exchange of views on major international developments.

Jaishankar said he conveyed Prime Minister Narendra Modi's warm wishes to his British counterpart and discussed ways to further advance economic cooperation between India and the United Kingdom.

Focus on Expanding India's Global Partnerships

The meetings at CHOGM 2026 reflect India's continued engagement with Commonwealth countries across infrastructure, trade, healthcare, defence and digital technology.

The $500 million credit facility and three new agreements with Guyana provide a framework for expanding bilateral cooperation, while discussions with Australian and British leaders underline India's broader efforts to strengthen economic and diplomatic partnerships.
