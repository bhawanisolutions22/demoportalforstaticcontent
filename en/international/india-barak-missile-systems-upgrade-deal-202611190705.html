India Signs ₹1,976 Crore Barak Missile Upgrade Deal

Posted Date 19 Nov 2026

India's Ministry of Defence has signed a ₹1,976.40 crore contract with Israel Aerospace Industries (IAI) to upgrade the Barak Point Defence Missile Systems installed on Indian Navy warships. The agreement focuses on modernising existing systems and addressing equipment obsolescence to maintain their operational effectiveness.

The contract was signed in New Delhi on Wednesday in the presence of Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh. The procurement has been finalised under the Other Capital Procurement Procedure.

Hardware and Software Modernisation

Under the agreement, the existing Barak Point Defence Missile Systems will receive comprehensive hardware and software upgrades. The programme is designed to resolve obsolescence-related challenges while introducing enhanced features to improve the systems' overall performance.

The modernisation will help ensure that the missile systems remain effective against evolving aerial threats and meet the operational requirements of the Indian Navy.

Strengthening the Indian Navy's Air Defence Capabilities

The Ministry of Defence stated that the upgrade will enhance the air defence capabilities of Indian Navy platforms. These systems play an important role in protecting naval vessels against potential aerial attacks.

By upgrading the existing Barak missile systems, India aims to strengthen the defensive capabilities of its warships and improve their preparedness to respond to emerging security challenges.

The ₹1,976.40 crore agreement with Israel Aerospace Industries represents an investment in maintaining and modernising the Indian Navy's existing defence infrastructure.
