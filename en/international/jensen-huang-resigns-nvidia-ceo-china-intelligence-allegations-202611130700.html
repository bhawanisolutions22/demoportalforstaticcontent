Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO Amid Allegations Involving Suspected China Intelligence Links

Posted Date 13 Nov 2026

Jensen Huang has stepped down as chief executive officer of Nvidia, bringing an abrupt end to his long tenure at the company he transformed into one of the world's leading suppliers of artificial intelligence chips. Nvidia confirmed on November 12 that Huang had resigned as president, CEO and a member of the board, with immediate effect.

The resignation follows growing scrutiny surrounding reports of Huang's personal relationship with a woman identified in media coverage as Alice Huang, who has reportedly attracted the attention of US intelligence agencies over suspected links to China's Ministry of State Security. The allegations have not been established publicly, and Beijing has denied claims of an intelligence connection.

Nvidia's board appointed Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress as interim CEO while it begins the search for a permanent successor. Kress, who joined Nvidia in 2013, will continue to oversee the company's financial operations alongside her temporary leadership responsibilities. The board has also appointed Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever chair.

Allegations Bring National Security Questions Into Focus

The controversy has drawn attention from the technology industry, US policymakers and national security officials. Media reports have linked Alice Huang to Singapore-based company Megaspeed, which has been described as an intermediary involved in the purchase of advanced chips. Allegations that the company was used to bypass US export restrictions have added to concerns surrounding the case.

Reports have said US intelligence agencies examined Alice Huang's possible connections to Chinese authorities. Secretary of State Marco Rubio was reportedly among the officials who raised concerns with President Donald Trump. China has rejected the allegations, while the claims involving Alice Huang remain subject to scrutiny.

Nvidia has said its internal review found no reason to believe that national security information or company secrets had been compromised. The company has not indicated that sensitive technology was transferred to China through the alleged relationship.

US Chip Export Policy Adds to the Debate

The developments have also renewed debate in Washington over the export of advanced artificial intelligence chips to China. Huang had previously argued that US technology companies should retain access to the Chinese market, warning that restrictions could strengthen overseas competitors.

The Trump administration has eased restrictions on exports of certain lower-tier Nvidia chips to China. The policy has exposed differences among US officials over how to balance commercial opportunities, technological competition and national security concerns.

President Trump had publicly expressed support for Huang before the resignation, describing him as an honourable person. Reports also said Huang told the president that he was unaware of any possible intelligence links involving Alice Huang.

Congress Seeks Further Information

The allegations have attracted attention from members of the US Congress. Reports indicate that the House Foreign Affairs Committee has sought a closed-door briefing from Huang and has considered using a subpoena to secure his appearance.

The matter could receive further congressional scrutiny as lawmakers examine the relationship between technology exports, national security and US competition with China. Any conclusions will depend on the findings of the relevant investigations and briefings.

Leadership Transition at a Critical Time for Nvidia

Huang co-founded Nvidia and led its transformation from a graphics-chip company serving the gaming market into a major supplier of processors used in artificial intelligence, data centres and advanced computing. Its products are central to the infrastructure used by leading AI developers and technology companies.

His departure creates a significant leadership transition for the company. Huang had been closely involved in Nvidia's strategy and operations, while Kress now faces the task of maintaining business continuity, guiding the company's expansion and overseeing competition in the fast-growing AI chip market.

The board has said it will consider both internal and external candidates for the permanent CEO position. Kress is expected to lead the company until that process is completed.

Nvidia Shares and the Wider AI Market

Huang's exit has added uncertainty for investors at a time when Nvidia's performance has been closely watched across global financial markets. Reports said the company's shares declined sharply during the period surrounding the leadership change, following a substantial fall from their earlier record level.

Nvidia's market position means that developments at the company can influence sentiment across the wider technology sector. Its chips are widely used in AI systems, and a prolonged period of uncertainty could affect suppliers, customers and investors with exposure to the industry.

The broader implications will depend on how smoothly Nvidia manages the transition and whether investigations find evidence of any breach involving confidential information or export-control rules. For now, the company faces the twin challenges of restoring investor confidence and maintaining its position in the global AI market.
