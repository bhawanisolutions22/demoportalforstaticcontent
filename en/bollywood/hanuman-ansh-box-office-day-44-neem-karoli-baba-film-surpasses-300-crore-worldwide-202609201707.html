Hanuman Ansh Box Office Day 44: Neem Karoli Baba Film Surpasses ₹300 Crore Worldwide

Hanuman Ansh continues its remarkable theatrical run, recording a sharp rise in collections on its seventh Saturday.

The Shobhinaw Satyaa and Chandan Anand starrer earned ₹9.75 crore net in India on Day 44, a 95% jump from the ₹5 crore collected on Day 43. With this, the film’s India net total stands at ₹258.13 crore after 44 days.

Across 7,119 shows nationwide, the film achieved an overall occupancy of 30%, with evening and night screenings crossing 42% and 45% respectively. Overseas markets contributed an additional ₹1.50 crore, taking the worldwide gross to ₹329.30 crore, including ₹304.80 crore from India.

Inspired by the life of Neem Karoli Baba, the film is based on Divine Detour: That Changed My Life. Set in pre‑Independence India, it follows Lakshmi Narayan’s spiritual journey that ultimately transforms him into Neem Karoli Baba.

The cast features Shobhinaw Satyaa, Chandan Anand, Anil Rastogi, Sugandha Malhotra, Bhagvandas Patel, and Neha Paranjpe. The film’s sustained momentum highlights its strong audience connect and growing box office legacy.
